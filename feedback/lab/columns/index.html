What's different

  1. Side by side. Every view is a column, so comparing games or kinds never needs a click. Columns scroll on their own.
  2. Nothing leaves the deck. A bug opens to its posts, a video to Claude's summary, right where it sits.
  3. Size says how much it matters. Reviews, threads, videos and refund notes are full cards, replies one line, YouTube comments a count on their video.

Where things went

  • All games home is the deck: By game or By kind. Live streams sit in Needs attention.
  • Bugs, Ideas, Loved, Media, Sales, Updates are a game's preset columns. Click a game chip.
  • All posts is the Reviews & threads column.
  • Achievements, Steam stats, Curators, Bundles, Competitors are columns under + Column.
  • Move, widen and remove columns from ⋯ or by dragging. The layout is remembered in this browser.
